## First-pass read

- **The top 5 all share a pattern**: a *physical, local, invisible-or-ignored* problem + a *phone/sensor* that makes it measurable + *data that flows to an institution that already exists*. That's what separates them from the "app that motivates people" pile.
- **Sustainability has less competition in judges' memory** (last season was sport-only, so every returning school has sport ideas in the drawer). This slightly favours a sustainability pick.
- Top 5 go to [`deep-dives/`](deep-dives/) for evidence, prior art, feasibility, legal risk, and red-teaming.
