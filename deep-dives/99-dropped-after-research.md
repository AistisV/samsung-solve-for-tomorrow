# Ideas that dropped after deeper research

Recorded so we don't re-explore them blindly.

## Inclusive PE co-pilot (was #3, 82 → ~70)
- **Smaller problem than assumed**: officially only **1.5%** of children are in the "preparatory" PE group, and the "special" group is smaller still ([Higienos institutas](https://vssis.hi.lt/Naujienos/Naujiena?NewsId=1405)). The exemption abuse is real but anecdotal and hard to quantify.
- **Prior art**: AI tools for adapted PE already exist ([ConnectedPE Adapted PE tool](https://connectedpe.com/tools), ChatGPE, PE GPTs). An LLM wrapper is not differentiated.
- Keep as a *feature* idea if we ever build something for PE teachers.

## Hogweed Hunter (was #5, 80 → ~70)
- **Lithuania already has a reporting app** (NMA Agro, used by the Ministry of Environment) and an official invasive-species register (**INVA**, inva.biip.lt) ([LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/1068970/sosnovskio-barscio-augimo-vietoms-pranesti-speciali-mobilioji-programele), [INVA](https://inva.biip.lt/naudinga-informacija/)).
- The remaining gap (regrowth tracking after removal) is real but narrow. Drone-AI detection is technically impressive but costly, and the removal itself is manual work, not a tech problem.

## Beetle Watch (satellite alerts for private forest owners, ~74)
- Detecting the early "green attack" stage from Sentinel-2 is a hard research problem (78% accuracy even in published studies).
- A commercial product already does it (**BugBit** by Slovenian start-up Yolanda, [EUSPA](https://www.euspa.europa.eu/newsroom-events/success-stories/yolanda-turning-satellite-data-early-warning-system-europes-forests)).
- Too ambitious for a school team to *demo credibly* in six weeks.

## Not revisited but still on the bench
- **Movement lab** (PE as self-experiment, 78): a great *educational* concept and cheap to demo. If the team insists on sport *and* on reaching inactive kids, this is the one to re-open.
- **Convince-the-block** (renovation vote AI, 78): strong local and policy logic, but the team would need access to heating-bill data and building owners. Harder to pilot in six weeks.
- **Fritidsbanken.lt** (sports gear library, 71): proven abroad, new to LT, but the tech is thin (lockers + app). More a social enterprise than a tech idea.
