# Idea pool — Sport & Technology

Theme: *How can tech make sport more accessible, more educational, more meaningful? How can tech get students more active?*

**Trap warning (see [past-winners](../research/past-winners.md))**: last season was sport-only and the finals were full of *"gamified app that gives points for moving / city quests / AR orienteering / screen-time-for-steps"*. Anything in that family starts with a novelty penalty.

Legend — Tech: AI / IoT / AR / VR / App / HW. Verdict: 🟢 shortlist · 🟡 maybe · 🔴 drop (reason).

## A. The "PE is broken" angle (school, universal reach)

| # | Idea | Tech | Local hook | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Exempt ≠ absent** — AI turns a doctor's PE restriction ("no running, no jumping") into a personalised alternative lesson plan the teacher can run *inside* the class. | AI (LLM + exercise DB) | PE exemption abuse; "preparatory group" certificates | 🟢 |
| S2 | **Inclusive PE co-pilot** — teacher describes the lesson + who's in class (wheelchair, asthma, obesity, broken arm) → AI generates adapted roles/rules so *everyone plays the same game*. | AI | Kids with disabilities "left on the bench" | 🟢 (merge with S1) |
| S3 | **Effort-not-talent grading** — cheap HR bands; grade = time in target zone, not sprint time. | IoT | Unsporty kids hate PE grades | 🔴 exists (Polar GoFit, MyZone) |
| S4 | **Movement lab** — every PE class runs a real experiment on itself: "does 10 min of X improve our reaction-time / memory score?" Phone app runs cognitive micro-tests before/after, pools anonymised class data nationally. Sport becomes *science you do on yourself*. | App + AI stats (+ watch HR) | ">50% of inactive kids don't see the point"; theme word *lavinantis* (educational) | 🟢 |
| S5 | **AI PE video coach** — phone films a skill (layup, forward roll); pose AI gives technique feedback to each student while teacher handles 30 kids. | AI vision | 1 teacher : 30 kids | 🟡 HomeCourt-ish; generic |
| S6 | **Active-break hallway** — projector + camera turn a corridor floor/wall into motion games during 15-min breaks. | AI pose + projector | Teens sit on phones during breaks | 🟡 exists commercially (Lü); DIY is cool for demo |
| S7 | **Posture & scoliosis self-screen** — phone camera back scan in annual health check. | AI vision | Annual *mokinio sveikatos pažyma* | 🟡 medical-device regulation risk |
| S8 | **ACL-Scan** — 30-sec phone video of a drop jump → AI measures knee valgus → flags high-risk landings → 10-min prevention warm-up plan for the team. Girls in basketball/volleyball have 2–8× ACL risk. | AI pose (MediaPipe) | Basketball nation; youth clubs; school health checks | 🟢 |
