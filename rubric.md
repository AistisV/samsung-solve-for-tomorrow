# Scoring rubric

Built from the official criteria. Every idea is scored 1–5 on each row; weighted total out of 100.

Stage 2 (local jury, the gate to the final) matters most — if we don't make TOP 5 in Lithuania, nothing else matters. Stage 3 criteria are folded in because a good idea should survive both.

| # | Criterion | Source | Weight | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T | **Technology fit** | 38.1, 48.1 | 3 | Tech is *essential* to the solution, not decoration. |
| B | **AI / IoT / VR / AR bonus** | 48.7 | 2 | One of these is core and natural, not bolted on. |
| C | **Creativity / novelty** | 38.2, 48.3 | 4 | A judge hasn't seen it before; clear gap vs. existing solutions. |
| Q | **Realism / feasibility** | 38.3, 48.4 | 4 | Clear causal model; doable with realistic resources; legal (GDPR, minors, safety). |
| L | **Local relevance** | 38.4 | 4 | Grounded in LT data, institutions, habits, climate, laws. |
| I | **Impact + scalability** | 38.6, 48.3, 48.5 | 3 | Measurable benefit; pattern transfers to other towns/countries; could influence policy. |
| R | **Reach to target audience** | 48.6 | 2 | Obvious distribution channel (schools, municipalities, existing apps/orgs). |
| D | **Demo-ability** | 38.5 (completeness) + our own | 2 | A team of 3–5 students can build a convincing prototype/pilot in ~6 weeks. |
| S | **Stickiness / one-liner** | our own | 1 | Explainable in one sentence; memorable; judges retell it. |

Weights sum to 25 → `total = Σ(score × weight) × 0.8` (max 100).

## Red flags (automatic heavy penalty)
- Generic "app that motivates people to X" with gamification and nothing else.
- Already exists and works well (unless we have a sharp twist).
- Requires hardware/infrastructure a municipality would never buy.
- Collects sensitive data on minors without a clear lawful basis.
- Theme-mismatch or tries to fit both themes.
