# Proof sheet: young drivers × wildlife collisions (Lithuania)

Checked on 2 Nov 2026 through web search snippets. ✅ = in an official or primary source · 🟡 = in a secondary source; check before quoting.

## The problem is big and growing
- ✅ **Police: 7,604 collisions with wild animals in 2025, ~21 a day, up from 2,422 in 2017 (3× in 8 years).** Riskiest months: May, Oct, Nov, Dec; those four months alone had 3,417, about half the year. Animals are most active ~17:00–23:00. [policija.lrv.lt, 26 Oct 2026](https://policija.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos/kasdien-daugiau-nei-20-susidurimu-su-zverimis-primename-apie-pavojus-rudeni-2tL5/), [15min](https://www.15min.lt/naujiena/aktualu/nusikaltimaiirnelaimes/susidurimo-su-laukiniais-gyvunais-rizika-kasdien-po-21-avarija-neisvengta-ir-zmoniu-zuciu-59-2792448)
  - Note: older figures we used (342 → 1,000) seem to count only some kinds of registered incidents; insurer ERGO counts 1,061 insured claims in 2025. **Use the police 7,604.**
- ✅ **Survey (Spinter for ERGO, Aug 2025, n=1,011, ages 18–75): 13% of drivers have had an accident caused by a wild animal, and another 14% only just avoided one.** No age breakdown published. [Spinter](https://spinter.lt/en/tyrimas-atskleide-kas-astuntas-vairuotojas-yra-patyres-avarija-del-laukinio-gyvuno/)
- 🟡 Roe deer are 53% of cases (BTA, 2025); the average claim is ~€2.6k; moose cause the biggest losses, up to €48k. [Reidas](https://reidasofficial.lt/eismas/susidurimai-su-laukiniais-gyvunais-lietuvoje-brangsta-viena-akimirka-gali-kainuoti-desimtis-tukstanciu-euru/), [infoerdve](https://infoerdve.lt/susidurimai-su-zverimis-kelyje-nuostoliai-siekia-ir-48-000-euru/)
- ✅ Moose cause 66.7% of the deaths in wildlife accidents (research). [PubMed 38791668](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38791668/)
- 🟡 Moose are hard to see because headlights don't light up their eyes, which sit high (LAUGAS coverage). [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/eismas/7/2842083/lietuvos-keliuose-ismani-sistema-padedanti-isvengti-susidurimo-su-gyvunais)

## Young and novice drivers are the risk group
- ✅ **TKA national report (2017–2020): 19–23-year-olds were ~15% of drivers at fault in accidents, and 55% of them had under 2 years' driving experience.** [TKA PDF](https://tka.lt/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2017-2020_EI-leidinys.pdf). Newer editions: [TKA catalogue](https://tka.lt/katalogas/eismo-ivykiu-statistika-lietuvoje/)
- 🟡 "Pradedantysis vairuotojas" means under 2 years' experience (cat. A/B). Limits: 0‰ alcohol, lower speed limits (70 km/h on ordinary roads, 90 on expressways; sources disagree whether it's 90 or 100 on motorways), and licence loss for offences that only bring a fine for others. **Check the motorway figure in the official KET text before quoting.** [Reidas](https://reidasofficial.lt/patarimai-ir-rekomendacijos/vairuotojo-pazymejimas/pradedanciojo-vairuotojo-baudos-apribojimai-ank-416/)
- ❌ **No public statistic links young drivers specifically to wildlife collisions.** Ask the police, the TKA or the insurers' association, or make it your own survey finding.

## Data you can build on
- ✅ **Mendeley dataset (Kučas, 2021): 14,989 ungulate–vehicle collisions in LT, 2002–2017, with year, month, day, hour, weekday, species, and sunrise/sunset hours. No coordinates listed.** Enough for an AI "when is it most dangerous" model. [Mendeley](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/4y8t78dsxy/2)
- ✅ Hotspot locations were mapped in a related study (fencing and hotspots, Land 2021, +16.4% a year in 2002–2017); coordinates would have to be requested from the author. [MDPI Land](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/10/4/338)
