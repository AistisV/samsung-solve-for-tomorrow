## Update (Nov 2026): BriedisStop prior art found in Lithuania
A KTU startup, **Dok inovacija**, has built **LAUGAS**: autonomous, solar-powered infrared sensor posts that detect warm, moving objects at the road edge at night and switch on driver warning lights. It is installed on a ~800 m state-road section, has a cooperation agreement with Via Lietuva, and integration talks were reported as ongoing. Sources: [KTU](https://ktu.edu/news/lietuvos-keliuose-ismani-sistema-pades-isvengti-susidurimo-su-gyvunais/), [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/eismas/7/2842083/lietuvos-keliuose-ismani-sistema-padedanti-isvengti-susidurimo-su-gyvunais), [bznstart](https://bznstart.lt/verslas/startuolis/startuolis-dok-inovacija-ismanus-sprendimas-kuris-gali-tapti-prioritetu-lietuvos-keliuose/), [KTU EEF project](https://eef.ktu.edu/projects/ismaniuju-technologiju-irenginys-tamsiuoju-paros-metu-perspejantis-vairuotojus-nuo-susidurimo-su-laukiniais-gyvunais/). The articles span several years, and no independent accuracy evaluation or national rollout was found.
Via Lietuva's own measures are physical (900+ km of fences, underpasses, green bridges); it also has a monitoring camera on the Via Baltica green bridge ([Via Lietuva](https://vialietuva.lt/naujienos/via-lietuva-atskleide-kokie-gyvunai-jau-naudojasi-via-baltica-magistraleje-esanciu-zaliuoju-tiltu)).
**Impact:** "detect + warn" is no longer new in LT. 38.2 (creativity) drops from 4 to about 2–3, so the stage-2 total falls from 26 to about 24. Remaining differentiation: AI species ID and false-alarm filtering (LAUGAS appears to trigger on any warm moving object, unverified), plus automatic crossing counts for fences and green bridges.
