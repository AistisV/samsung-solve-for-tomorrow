# Round 5 brief: find something genuinely GOOD (not safe-and-lame)

## Why round 5 exists
Round 4 ([`../round4/RESULTS.md`](../round4/RESULTS.md)) optimised for the "winning formula" and produced safe, average ideas. The team's verdict, in their own words:
- **Classroom CO₂ meter:** "really not good, I've seen it personally so many times", even with Samsung hardware.
- **HeartSquad (CPR trainer):** "used like once".
- **RecessBox (break-time kit locker):** "used by who? no one".
- **ShowerCoach (live € shower meter):** "cool, I'd lowk have it in my bath" (their dad complains about wasted hot water), but "not an SfT project?". It's the only idea that got a *want-it* reaction.

**Lesson:** formula-checking converges on the average. Round 5 filters for **"would a 16-year-old be proud and excited to build this, and would a real person actually WANT to use it repeatedly?"** The winning formula in `../round4/BRIEF.md` still matters (relatable, prototype, AI, pilot, LT hook), but it's a tie-breaker, not the generator.

## Contest (short)
Samsung Solve for Tomorrow 2026 Baltics; team of 3–5, grades 9–12, Lithuania; can code and build hardware. Theme: **Sport & tech** (make sport accessible/educational/meaningful; get students active) **or Sustainability** (tech for a greener tomorrow). Idea form Oct 16; concept + presentation Nov 6; Baltic final in December, pitched in English. Bonus for AI/IoT/VR/AR. Generalist jury (Samsung staff, educators, NGOs).

## The new filters (score each 0–2; the first three matter most)
1. **WANT**: would a real person use or want this repeatedly, not once? ("I'd have it in my bath")
2. **WOW**: a stage demo or insight that makes people lean forward; the team would be proud to explain it to friends.
3. **NOT-SEEN**: not a staple of school science fairs (CO₂ lights, smart bins, step apps, plant-watering, AI form-check, recycling quizzes, food-waste scales).
4. **Buildable** by teens in 6 weeks with a working demo (hardware welcome).
5. **AI/IoT does real work** (not a wrapper around a photo or an LLM chat).
6. **Big enough**: it scales beyond one family or school, so it feels like a "solve for tomorrow" project, not a gadget.
7. **LT hook**: a Lithuanian fact, habit or culture that makes the jury nod (basketball, lakes, forests, mushrooms, old Soviet blocks, sodybos, the Baltic sea, storks, winter darkness, etc.).
