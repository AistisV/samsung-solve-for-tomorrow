# Lithuanian facts & hooks

Raw material for ideas. Each fact is a potential "why this matters here" line for criterion 38.4 (local relevance).

## Sport / physical activity

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Only **~5%** of grade 9–13 students in LT get enough daily physical activity (HBSC) — **among the 5 least active countries in Europe**, and trending down. | [MadeinVilnius](https://madeinvilnius.lt/en/life/health/young-people-in-Lithuania-are-among-the-least-physically-active-in-Europe/) |
| 13.6% of children active ≥60 min after school (Hygiene Institute 2020). | [SAM](https://sam.lrv.lt/lt/veiklos-sritys/visuomenes-sveikatos-prieziura/mityba-ir-fizinis-aktyvumas-2/sveikatinamasis-fizinis-aktyvumas/fizinio-aktyvumo-situacija/) |
| >50% of non-sporty kids say they **don't see the point / don't feel the need** → the problem is *meaning* and *physical literacy*, not access alone. | [NSA](https://www.ltusportas.lt/beveik-puse-lietuvos-gyventoju-yra-reguliariai-fiziskai-aktyvus-vaikai-ir-paaugliai-aktyvesni/) |
| **PE exemption abuse**: doctors report many kids request certificates for the "preparatory group" so they "don't have to run or do push-ups". | [tv3.lt](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/lietuva/vaikui-susirgus-pakanka-tevu-rastelio-o-kas-atleis-nuo-kuno-kulturos-n1011470), [ve.lt](https://ve.lt/naujienos/lietuva/lietuvos-naujienos/mokiniu-baubas---kuno-kultura-) |
| **NVŠ voucher**: state pays €15–25/month (up to €50 for SEN / social support) toward an accredited after-school activity. From Aug 2026, applications go via **CPIS / mokausi.lt**. Some families still don't use it. | [SKM](https://www.skm.lt/naujienos/skm-naujienos/n/20262027-mokslo-metu-naujoves-priemimas-i-nvs-programas-per-cpis-ir-neformaliojo-vaiku-svietimo-krepselis-4452/), [Sportas24](https://sportas24.lt/kuluaruose/vaiku-sportas-uz-kelis-eurus-per-menesi-kodel-dalis-seimu-nepasinaudoja-nvs-finansavimu/) |
| Kids driven to school **+200% over 30 years**; 5/10 travel independently vs 7/10 in the 1990s. 182 children injured within 100 m of schools 2022–25. | [Vilnius](https://vilnius.lt/naujienos/tyrimas-apie-vaiku-sauguma-kelyje-susimasto-tik-2-tevai-is-10), [15min](https://www.15min.lt/verslas/naujiena/saugukelyje-lt/stebejo-vaikus-ir-ju-tevus-tyrimas-atskleide-kaip-vaikai-is-tiesu-elgiasi-kelyje-1662-2754846) |
| Teens 14–17: **4.5 h/day online on school days, ~6 h weekends**; 60% of grades 9–12 exceed 4 h; **56% want to cut down**. | [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/svietimas/45/2762090/ekranu-laika-apkarpes-10-okas-zaidimai-savaitgaliais-darbo-diena-100-proc-mokslo) |
| School stadiums: renovated with state money, but access after hours is inconsistent (Panevėžys residents: "only for the chosen ones?"). | [jp.lt](https://jp.lt/panevezieciai-piktinasi-ar-mokyklu-stadionai-tik-isrinktiesiems/) |
| Kids with disabilities: lack of adapted sport; "still cases where kids are left on the bench". | [Sportas.lt](https://www.sportas.lt/naujiena/542471/vaiku-su-negalia-sporto-uzkulisiai-trenere-atvirai-prabilo-apie-issukius) |
| LT has the EU's highest suicide rate (~23/100k); youth loneliness/stress high. Physical activity is a known protective factor. | [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/health/2025/10/11/suicide-rate-in-lithuania-remains-one-of-the-highest-in-the-eu-despite-previous-declines) |
| Long dark winters (sunset ~16:00 in Dec) — outdoor activity collapses Nov–Mar. | common knowledge |
| Basketball is quasi-religion; strong federations (LKF), huge club culture. | common knowledge |

## Sustainability

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| **Private wells**: ~1 in 3 tested wells has nitrate contamination; early 2024: 51% of boreholes & 45% of wells failed hygiene norms. Nitrates are **invisible, tasteless, odourless**, dangerous for infants & pregnancy. Free tests only for pregnant/infant households. | [NVSC](https://nvsc.lrv.lt/lt/veiklos-sritys/visuomenes-sveikatos-saugos-uztikrinimas/suliniu-vandens-tyrimai-del-nitritu-nitratu/), [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/sveikata/682/2502311/gyventojai-raginami-tirti-vandens-is-suliniu-kokybe-uzterstas-vanduo-pavojingas-sveikatai) |
| **Sosnowsky's hogweed**: invasive, causes burns; removal "inconsistent, keeps regrowing"; private owners don't act; range still growing. 166 ha cleared in state forests 2025; **national 2025–2029 programme** now funds private owners too. Public asked to report sightings. | [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/2303244/stoja-i-kova-su-sosnovskio-barsciais-naikinamas-nenuosekliai-vel-atsinaujina), [tv3](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/gyvenimas/pamacius-si-augala-nedelsiant-praneskite-kreipiasi-i-visus-n1357497) |
| **Spruce bark beetle** (žievėgraužis tipografas): ~1.0 M m³ spruce damaged 2023, 0.7 M m³ 2024; 2nd generation growing 4 years in a row; hot dry summers → record risk. | [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/mokslas-ir-it/11/2529680/siemet-prognozuojama-dar-didesne-zievegrauzio-tipografo-populiacija), [AM](https://am.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos/zievegrauzis-tipografas-dar-aktyviai-grauzia-egles-del-sausu-oru-dar-tinkamas-metas-jas-isvezti/) |
| **Textile waste** separate collection mandatory since 2025-01-01; 1,700+ containers; network insufficient; contamination of containers; fines €30–600. | [AM](https://am.lrv.lt/lt/veiklos-sritys-1/atlieku-politika/tekstiles-atliekos/), [MadeinVilnius](https://madeinvilnius.lt/transportas/tekstiles-atlieku-problemos-lietuvoje-truksta-konteineriu-perdirbimo-galimybes-nera-pakankamai-isvystytos/) |
| **Deposit system** returns ~90% of containers — held up as EU model. Proof LT *can* change habits with the right mechanism. | [jp.lt](https://jp.lt/per-metus-lietuvoje-grazinta-daugiau-kaip-655-mln-uzstato-pakuociu/) |
| **Old phones**: 53% of Lithuanians put old phone in a drawer, only 9% recycle; LT misses the EU 65% e-waste collection target. | [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/mokslas-ir-it/11/2992310/europieciu-namuose-beveik-700-mln-senu-telefonu-kur-priduoti-nereikalinga-elektronika) |
| **Solar boom**: 161k+ prosumers (incl. remote solar parks); solar = 17.75% of LT electricity 2025. Time-of-use tariffs (1/2/4 zones); storage fee changes 2026. | [Wikipedia LT](https://lt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saul%C4%97s_energetika_Lietuvoje), [ESO](https://www.eso.lt/namams/elektra/tarifu-planai-kainos-atsiskaitymas/gaminanciu-vartotoju-atsiskaitymo-budai-2026-metais/4829) |
| **Wood/solid-fuel heating smoke** in small towns & private-house districts: PM2.5 spikes every heating season; wet wood & waste burning are key causes. | [NVSC via Jonavos žinios](https://www.jonavoszinios.lt/naujiena/nvsc-oro-tarsa-sildymo-sezono-metu-kuo-kenksmingi-dumai) |
| **Curonian Lagoon & Baltic**: cyanobacteria blooms each summer; bathing advice at >20k cells/ml, ban at >100k. | [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/sveikata/682/2983073/baltijos-juroje-ir-kursiu-mariose-zydi-melsvabakteres-raginama-nesimaudyti) |
| Food waste ~80k t/yr of food residue; schools waste most in kitchens/canteens (prepared-not-served, served-not-eaten). | [SAM](https://sam.lrv.lt/lt/veiklos-sritys/visuomenes-sveikatos-prieziura/mityba-ir-fizinis-aktyvumas-2/vaiku/svedisko/), [KurkLT review](https://data.kurklt.lt/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/MAISTO-SVAISTYMO-APZVALGA.pdf) |

## Cross-cutting hooks worth exploiting
- **Institutions that could adopt a solution**: municipalities, NVSC (public health), Aplinkos apsaugos departamentas, Valstybinių miškų urėdija (VMU), schools/ŠMSM, NVŠ system (mokausi.lt), užstato sistema (USAD), ESO, sports federations (LKF), LSU (Sports University), Higienos institutas.
- **Existing national data systems** we could plug into: NVŠ/CPIS, e-dienynas (Tamo/Eduka), Gamtos stebėsena / biomon.lt, invazinių rūšių registration, oro kokybė (AAA monitoring stations).
- **Samsung-native angles**: Galaxy phone camera + on-device AI, SmartThings IoT, Galaxy Watch health sensors, Galaxy phone recycling / "Galaxy Upcycling" (old phones as IoT sensors!).
