## 0. The strongest local finding

**Lithium batteries in household waste are setting Lithuania's waste system on fire, and the trend is rising fast.**

- Fire and Rescue Department (PAGD) data: battery-related fires went **12 (2023) → 21 (2024) → 33 (2025)**, almost 3× in two years. Note that this counts all battery fires, including e-scooters, not only fires in waste. ([jp.lt / eei.lt via search](https://jp.lt/elektronikos-atliekos-auganti-rizika-kaip-isvengti-gaisru/), [alkas.lt](https://alkas.lt/2026/07/17/30-proc-gyventoju-netinkamai-tvarko-smulkiaja-elektronika/))
- 2025: **"at least five fires in waste sorting companies in three months"**. The **Energesman** sorting plant near Vilnius burned in April 2025 (~7,500 m² of building lost). The **Kaunas CHP (KKJ)** fuel bunker caught fire on **8 May 2025**, and preliminary data point to lithium batteries. A battery-sorting warehouse in Paneriai also burned. ([15min](https://www.15min.lt/verslas/naujiena/mokslas-it/licio-baterijos-nematoma-gresme-kodel-daugeja-gaisru-1290-2521956), [kasvyksta Kaunas](https://kaunas.kasvyksta.lt/2025/05/08/112/pranesama-apie-gaisra-kauno-kogeneracineje-jegaineje-is-pastato-verziasi-dumai/), [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/lietuvoje/2/2593058/vel-registruotas-gaisras-atlieku-imoneje-vilniuje-dege-licio-baterijos))
- A. Skinulis (Environmental Protection Institute), in an LRT op-ed: **"nine fires at waste management plants this year"**, the latest in Kėdainiai. *(The op-ed's year was not visible in the snippet; probably 2025.)* ([LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/verslo-pozicija/692/2697201/alfredas-skinulis-penki-pakeitimai-padesiantys-stabdyti-gaisrus-atlieku-tvarkymo-imonese))
- Garbage trucks catch fire too. An **Ecoservice** truck carrying municipal waste burned on Laisvės pr., Vilnius, from discarded batteries. Other cases: "workers heard an explosion" in a Vilnius truck, and Ekobazė's facility. ([15min](https://www.15min.lt/naujiena/aktualu/nusikaltimaiirnelaimes/vilniuje-staiga-uzsiliepsnojo-siuksles-siuksliavezeje-59-1905194), [MadeinVilnius](https://madeinvilnius.lt/naujienos/miestas/vilniuje-siuksliavezeje-kilo-gaisras-nufilmuota-kaip-dega-ir-sprogineja/), [tv3](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/lietuva/vilniuje-uzsidege-atlieku-rusiavimo-irenginys-itaria-kad-kaltininke-licio-baterija-n1546968))
- Behaviour: **1 in 3 residents mishandles or hoards small electronics with built-in batteries**, and the average resident owns about 10 such devices. Home hoarding of batteries has tripled (3% → 9%), while throwing them in the bin fell 30% over five years (Spinter survey for Žalvaris). ([alkas.lt](https://alkas.lt/2026/07/17/30-proc-gyventoju-netinkamai-tvarko-smulkiaja-elektronika/), [Spinter](https://spinter.lt/en/lietuvoje-bateriju-atlieku-tvarkymo-iprociai-keiciasi-maziau-ismeta-netinkamai-bet-dazniau-kaupia-namuose/))
- **Disposable vapes:** separate treatment costs **5× more** than other small electronics, so nobody funds separate collection. The Ministry of Environment is negotiating with vape sellers. ([alkas.lt / AM](https://am.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos/nebetinkamas-naudoti-elektronines-cigaretes-privalu-rusiuoti-su-elektros-ir-elektronines-irangos-atliekomis/), [eei.lt](https://eei.lt/surenkama-ir-perdirbama-per-mazai-elektroniniu-cigareciu-atlieku-tvarkymo-issukiai-lietuvoje))
- **Policy deadline:** under the EU Battery Regulation, LT must collect **63% of portable batteries by end-2027 and 73% by 2030**. Today the target is 45%, met with roughly 500–600 t/yr. ([AM](https://am.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos/isigaliojes-naujasis-es-bateriju-reglamentas-uztikrins-tvaru-visa-bateriju-gyvavimo-cikla/), [ukininkopatarejas](https://ukininkopatarejas.lt/naujienos/es-bateriju-reglamentas-kokia-nauda-vartotojui/))

Other useful LT waste data:
- **VAATC "Rūšiavimo indeksas"** (Vilnius region sorting index): published quarterly since 2019, based on **manual morphology (composition) sampling** at the MBT plants. 2025 figures: about **240 kg of mixed waste per person per year**, made up of **31.9% packaging (76.5 kg), 19.3% food (46.3 kg), 8.1% textiles (19.4 kg)** and 40.7% residual. Autumn 2025 set a **record for textile in mixed waste** and a record low for food waste. ([ekonomika.lt](https://ekonomika.lt/vilniaus-regionas-rusiuoja-daugiau-taciau-viena-atlieku-rusis-misriuose-konteineriuose-pasieke-rekorda-9363/), [MadeinVilnius](https://madeinvilnius.lt/naujienos/lietuvos-naujienos/rusiavimo-indeksas-atskleide-vilnieciai-meta-240-kg-atlieku-per-metus/), [VAATC](https://www.vaatc.lt/sumazejo-maisto-atlieku-auga-samoningumas-ar-tusteja-pinigine/))
- In 2023, **66% of municipal waste was still mixed**. ([EEA 2025 country profile](https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/topics/in-depth/waste-and-recycling/municipal-and-packaging-waste-management-country-profiles/lt-municipal-waste-factsheet.pdf/@@download/file))
- The Vilnius CHP burns up to **200 kt/yr** of residual waste from the Vilnius and Utena regions. ([LT Wikipedia](https://lt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilniaus_kogeneracin%C4%97_j%C4%97gain%C4%97))
- **Textiles:** collected volume rose about 10% from 2024 to 2025, but containers get items unfit for reuse. LT's first household-textile centre (~€5M, >20 kt/yr, with an **automatic fibre-sorting robot**) is being built in Vilnius. Recycling capacity today is "limited". ([Structum](https://structum.lt/5-mln-euru-investicija-i-tekstiles-atlieku-perdirbima-vilniuje-iskils-naujas-atlieku-tvarkymo-centras/), [15min](https://www.15min.lt/verslas/naujiena/bendroves/5-mln-euru-investicija-i-tekstiles-perdirbima-vilniuje-bus-statomas-naujas-centras-663-2768764))
- **Bulky-waste dumping at container sites:** Vilnius (VASA) already runs about 70 cameras, 50+ audio units and 3 patrol drones, with fines up to €600 (€1,400 in some reports). → **This niche is already occupied.** ([LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/verslas/4/1275019/vilniuje-salia-konteineriu-irengtos-kameros-uzfiksavo-daugiau-kaip-70-pazeideju-baudos-gali-siekti-iki-600-euru), [Kauno diena](https://klaipeda.diena.lt/naujienos/vilnius/miesto-pulsas/vilniuje-uz-ne-vietoje-paliktas-siuksles-baudos-iki-14-tukst-euru-pazeidejai-stebimi-ir-dronais-1164069))
- **Deposit system:** 9 of 10 containers are returned. Refillable glass is about 10% of deposit packaging. PPWR requires **10% reusable beverage packaging by 2030** and 40% of transport packaging reusable by 2030. ([Wikipedia LT](https://lt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienkartini%C5%B3_g%C4%97rim%C5%B3_pakuo%C4%8Di%C5%B3_u%C5%BEstato_sistema), [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en/insights/new-eu-packaging-and-packaging-waste-rules-10-key-things-every-global-business-should-know))
- **Bio-waste:** the Šiauliai region reports 1,021 t of food/kitchen waste collected in 2025 with "minimal impurities". → Contamination of bio-waste is **not** a strong LT pain point. ([ŠRATC](https://www.sratc.lt/maisto-ir-virtuves-atliekos/))

## 2. Top profiles

### No. 1: HopperGuard: a thermal + AI camera that catches battery fires inside garbage trucks and maps where batteries come from

**One-line pitch:** A €150 thermal + camera module over the garbage-truck hopper warns the driver seconds after a lithium battery starts overheating, and also maps which container sites the batteries come from, so the city puts collection boxes and outreach exactly there.

**Problem (LT evidence):** battery-related fires 12 → 21 → 33 (2023–25, PAGD). At least 5 waste-plant fires in 3 months of 2025. Energesman (7,500 m² burned) and the KKJ bunker fire (8 May 2025, lithium batteries suspected). Ecoservice truck fires in Vilnius. One third of residents mishandle small electronics. The EU Battery Regulation requires 63% collection by 2027, up from 45% today. Sources are in Section 0.

**What the tech does:**
1. **Thermal channel:** a low-res thermal array (e.g. MLX90640, 32×24 px, or a Seek Thermal core, as in the Goodyear pilot) watches the hopper and compaction area. On-device logic flags a temperature anomaly (rapid rise or absolute threshold; Goodyear alarms at >200°F ≈ 93°C). The driver gets a cab alarm and GPS and can "hot-dump" the load safely before the whole truck burns.
2. **RGB AI channel:** a camera sees each container tip. A small object-detection model (YOLO-class, on a Raspberry Pi / Galaxy phone) spots batteries, vapes, power banks and small electronics.
3. **Map:** each detection is tagged with GPS and container-site ID, giving a heat map for the operator, municipality and EPR organisation. They then place battery boxes, target leaflets and campaigns, and track change over time.

**Collapse test:** nobody can see inside a hopper while it compacts. Nobody can inspect tens of thousands of container tips a day. Without the sensors there is no early warning and no attribution. **Passes.**

**Adoption loop:** *Who:* waste collection operators (e.g. Ecoservice, regional operators) and their drivers. *Why:* a truck fire destroys a truck and endangers the crew, and every load that reaches a plant carries fire risk. *Who pays:* the operator, and possibly insurers or battery EPR organisations (EPA / GIA), because the hotspot map directly serves the 63% collection target. *How it reaches them:* retrofit to the existing fleet, like a reversing camera. *First partner:* a regional waste management centre (RATC), a local collector, or the **EPA** electronics distributors' association, which already runs a **national environmental olympiad on batteries for school students** ([epa.lt](https://www.epa.lt/en/naujienos/nacionaline-aplinkosaugos-olimpiada-2025/)), a natural school link.

**Proven building blocks:**
- **Goodyear, Arizona + Seek Thermal:** thermal cameras on trash trucks alarm drivers at >200°F. It won a 2025 national Best Safety Innovation Award. ([Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2025/08/goodyear-launches-innovative-thermal-camera-system-to-ward-off-trash-truck-fires-caused-by-battery-disposal/), [City of Goodyear](https://www.goodyearaz.gov/Home/Components/News/News/13489/5609), [KJZZ](https://www.kjzz.org/kjzz-news/2025-08-15/as-more-garbage-trucks-catch-fire-over-lithium-ion-batteries-valley-cities-look-for-solutions))
- **AI truck cameras for contents:** Prairie Robotics runs in about 40 N. American cities, and Tacoma piloted it. ([Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/prairie-robotics-tacoma-ai-camera-technology-expansion/757521/), [Tacoma](https://tacoma.gov/news/city-of-tacomas-solid-waste-management-launches-smart-camera-pilot-to-reduce-contamination-in-recycling/))
- **AI battery detection in waste:** Visia AI reports 94–98% accuracy (X-ray), and PFU/Ricoh Raptor VISION BATTERY 94% (X-ray + AI, launched Oct 2025). This proves the detection concept, although our RGB version sees only visible items. ([Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/recycling/2025/08/26/visia-x-ray-tech-helps-prevent-mrf-battery-fires/), [PFU](https://www.pfu.ricoh.com/raptor-vision/battery/)); the research dataset BatSort ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.05802)).

**Prior art and how ours differs:** Goodyear does thermal alarms only, with no attribution. Prairie does contamination feedback for single-family curbside carts. X-ray systems are €100k-class plant equipment [unverified price class]. **Ours differs in three ways:** (a) it combines early warning and **source mapping** in one cheap retrofit; (b) it is designed for **shared container sites** (the LT/EU apartment-block reality), where feedback goes to the site, the apartment association (bendrija) and the collection-box network, not to individual households, which also avoids GDPR problems; (c) the output feeds the **63%-by-2027 battery collection target**. Not seen in LT.

**Measurable impact:**
- Batteries/vapes detected per 1,000 container tips, by site. Target: fewer at sites where boxes and leaflets are added, compared with control sites.
- Hot-spot alarms and hot-dumps (fires prevented). Truck and plant fire incidents (PAGD baseline: 33 battery fires in 2025, all causes).
- Portable-battery collection-rate share for the pilot municipality.

**Pilot by Nov 6 (realistic):**
1. Weeks 1–2: build the rig. Train the detector on public data (BatSort, TACO) plus the team's own photos of about 300 batteries, vapes and power banks mixed into clean packaging waste.
2. Weeks 2–4: **baseline audit.** With the teacher, gloves and a partner's permission, hand-sort a sample of packaging-container waste (e.g. 50–100 kg from the school or a partner site) and count batteries and vapes per kg. This gives **real LT pilot data** that nobody has published. Also measure model precision and recall on footage of that sample.
3. In parallel: request a letter of interest from a regional operator, RATC or EPA, and ask for 1–2 days of hopper footage or a ride-along. The thermal alarm is tested on the bench with a safe heat source (a heating pad or resistor). **Never with a real battery in thermal runaway.**

**Demo:** a clear acrylic "mini hopper" with the thermal + camera module and a live dashboard. (1) A student drops a vape into a pile of packaging, a box appears on screen and a pin lands on the Vilnius map. (2) A heated "fake battery" (resistor) is buried, and the thermal view turns red with a cab-style alarm within seconds. This is the wow moment.

**Top 3 judge objections:**
1. *"Thermal only sees the surface; the battery is buried."* The Goodyear deployment shows surface anomalies appear fast enough to warn drivers, because fire propagates upward. The RGB channel catches visible devices at the tip, before compaction. For the full answer, cite the complementary plant-side X-ray systems and position HopperGuard as the cheap first line.
2. *"Why won't people just be educated?"* Campaigns have run for years (AM, RATCs), yet fires tripled. Education without targeting is blind. The map tells the city *where*, and whether an intervention worked.
3. *"Trucks are dirty, wet and vibrating; students can't build that."* The product version uses IP67 automotive enclosures, as reversing cameras do. The student prototype proves the detection and alarm logic. Seek Thermal already sells ruggedised cores.

**Self-score:** Collapse 9 · Adoption 8 · Proven 8 · Measurable 8 · Not saturated 8 · Demo-able 9 · LT evidence 9 · (Bonus: AI + IoT core; policy hook = EU Battery Reg. 63%/2027.)

### No. 2: Live Sorting Index: continuous AI waste composition per truckload

**One-line pitch:** VAATC measures what Vilnians throw away by hand-sorting samples a few times a year. We put a camera + AI over the tipping floor or infeed belt to measure every truckload, so each district gets its own live sorting score.

**Problem:** 66% of LT municipal waste is still mixed (EEA 2025). In Vilnius mixed waste, 31.9% is packaging, 19.3% food and 8.1% textile (record high). The Sorting Index relies on **quarterly manual morphology sampling**, which is slow, expensive, small-sample and only region-wide. Nobody knows which districts or routes are improving. Sources are in Section 0.

**Tech:** a camera over the infeed conveyor or tipping zone at an MBT/sorting plant. A segmentation model classifies the visible stream into categories (plastic, paper, glass, textile, food, e-waste/batteries…) and estimates area/volume share. Each truck is matched to its route through the plant's weighbridge log. The output is a composition per route, shown on a district map and fed into the public Sorting Index.

**Collapse:** manual morphology cannot run on every load. **Passes.**

**Adoption loop:** VAATC / RATCs already own the metric and publish it, and municipalities use it for campaigns and PAYT fee design. Payment would come from VAATC's or the municipality's education/monitoring budget. First partner: **VAATC** (atliekukultura.lt).

**Proven:** Greyparrot (UK) AI waste-analytics cameras on sorting lines [vendor and details from background knowledge, unverified here]. Prairie Robotics / AI truck cameras. Visia real-time plant analytics ([Recycling Product News](https://www.recyclingproductnews.com/article/43846/visia-transforms-recycling-with-advanced-hazard-detection-and-real-time-insights)).

**Differs from prior art:** commercial systems optimise plant yield. Ours turns plant data into **public, district-level behaviour feedback**, a civic use. LT already has the public-index habit to plug into.

**Impact metric:** kg per person of recyclables in mixed waste, by district. Target: a measurable drop in districts that receive feedback.

**Pilot by Nov 6:** harder. It needs plant access. A fallback is to photograph and label bags from the school's mixed bin weekly and compare the AI estimate with hand-weighed composition (accuracy test).

**Risks:** plant access and safety; the camera sees only the top layer, which is why volume and area estimates must be calibrated against hand sorts; "VAATC is happy with quarterly data".

**Self-score:** Collapse 8 · Adoption 7 · Proven 7 · Measurable 8 · Not saturated 8 · Demo 7 · Evidence 9.

### No. 3: Container-site sorting score (packaging trucks)

**One-line pitch:** a camera on the packaging-collection truck grades every shared container site on contamination and prints a monthly "sorting score" sticker and QR report for the building. It is Prairie Robotics' approach adapted to Lithuania's shared bins.

**Problem:** contaminated separate fractions reduce recyclate quality (EEA profile). Shared apartment-block containers remove individual accountability. 66% of waste is mixed.

**Tech / collapse:** as in No. 1 (the same hardware). Every tip is classified as clean or contaminated, and scores are aggregated per site. No human can grade thousands of tips.

**Adoption:** the operator and municipality deploy it. Feedback goes to apartment associations and building administrators. The EPR packaging organisations (Žaliasis taškas, GIA) care about recyclate quality and could co-fund.

**Proven:** Prairie Robotics reports **25% (East Lansing) to 40%** contamination reduction with targeted feedback ([Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/ai-truck-mounted-cameras-contamination-identification-wm-the-recycling-partnership/711099/), [SuperAnnotate case](https://www.superannotate.com/blog/prairie-robotics-case-study)).

**Differs:** those systems work with individual carts. LT needs site-level social feedback (sticker and QR), which is untested but low-tech.

**Risks:** privacy concerns in US deployments ([Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/recycling-trucks-ai-powered-cameras-110535252.html)), answered by site-level aggregation and a hopper-only view. It is unproven that apartment-level feedback changes behaviour.

**Recommendation:** pitch it as **mode 2 of HopperGuard** ("the same camera, a second job"), not as a separate idea.

**Self-score:** Collapse 8 · Adoption 7 · Proven 8 · Measurable 8 · Not saturated 7 · Demo 8 · Evidence 7.

## 3. Strongest pick: **HopperGuard (No. 1), with the container-site score (No. 3) as its second mode**

Why:
- **Urgent, visible, growing problem with hard LT numbers:** fires 12→21→33, burning plants and trucks in Vilnius and Kaunas, and a 2027 EU deadline (63%).
- **Collapse test is airtight:** nobody can see inside a compacting hopper.
- **Deployment is realistic:** a few hundred trucks, not millions of bins, retrofitted like a reversing camera. There is a clear payer (the operator, whose truck is at risk) and a policy co-payer (battery EPR).
